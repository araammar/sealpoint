Project Brief
The Real Seal Co. · Binghamton, NY
FEATURED

A Salesforce-based operations system for a growing seal coating business.

SealPoint Consulting partnered with The Real Seal Co., a seal coating business based in Binghamton, NY, to design and implement a Salesforce-based operations management system. The goal of this project was to replace a manual and fragmented process for managing customers, scheduling jobs, and coordinating employees.

The solution centralizes all core business functions into a single platform, allowing for efficient tracking of customer information, job scheduling, and workforce management. By integrating reporting and dashboard capabilities, the system provides real-time insights into operations, revenue, and performance. This implementation enables The Real Seal to operate more efficiently, reduce errors, and scale its business with a structured and data-driven approach.
